Sign in however you like: **email**, a **social account** or a **wallet**. Your login is your identity. Credits you buy, from any wallet or chain, land in the account you are signed in to.

## Your built-in wallet

Every user gets a **non-custodial wallet on Base and Solana**, secured by Coinbase. It is created for you when you sign in, so you can hold and pay with stablecoins without installing a wallet app.

- **Non-custodial.** Singularity Layer never holds its keys.
- **Yours to take with you.** You can export the private key from your wallet settings.
- **Works next to your own wallets.** You can still connect an external wallet as well.

## Connecting an external wallet

In the dashboard, connect a **Solana** wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, detected automatically) or an **EVM** wallet (for Base and other EVM payments). You can sign in with it, or link it to the account you already use.

## Three ways to authenticate

  <Card title="Per-request signature" icon="key">
    Sign each request. Maximal statelessness; good for scripts and agents.

## Why wallets

- **No custody**: the network never holds your keys; you sign actions yourself.
- **No KYC**: a wallet or an email is all the identity needed.
- **Portable**: the same account works across Grid, Machines, Pods, Vault, credits and (for operators) staking.

## Sessions

A session token is issued after you sign a challenge and is valid for a limited time. It authorizes reads and credit-billed requests without re-signing each call. Reconnecting or signing again refreshes it.

<Note>For confidential Grid inference, wallet auth is separate from the **encryption** keys: your prompt is sealed to the node with ephemeral keys regardless of how you authenticate. See [Confidential compute](/cloud/concepts/confidential-compute).</Note>
