## The short version

You have wide creative freedom. We do not judge taste, style, or theme. Fiction, art, satire, horror, mature themes and adult-oriented art of **fictional adults** are allowed. A small set of things are never allowed, because they are illegal or cause real harm to real people. Those lines are not negotiable.

## Never allowed

1. **Sexual content involving minors, or anyone who looks like a minor.** Absolute. No exceptions, no "fictional" or "art" exception. Accounts are closed and we report to the authorities as the law requires.
2. **Sexual or intimate images of real, identifiable people** made without their consent, including realistic edits or face swaps of a real person into sexual content.
3. **Deceptive deepfakes of real people**: impersonation, fake evidence, fraud, harassment, election or political deception.
4. **Threats or incitement of violence** against real people or groups, and content that promotes terrorism or violent extremism.
5. **Forged or fraudulent documents**: fake IDs, passports, currency, official papers, counterfeit goods or packaging.
6. **Content that is illegal where you are, or where the node serving your request runs.** You are responsible for your own jurisdiction.
7. **Infringement**: do not use the service to copy someone else's copyrighted work or trademark in a way that violates their rights.

## Everything else is yours

Images you generate are yours. The model (FLUX.2 klein, Apache-2.0) places no ownership claim on outputs. You are responsible for how you use them.

## How requests are protected

Prompts and images are sealed end to end between you and the node that makes the image. Singularity Layer does not read them and cannot screen them in advance. That privacy is why these rules matter: we act on reports and abuse signals, not by reading your prompts.

- **Node operators** may not keep, inspect, copy, or share prompts or images, and may not use them for any purpose other than producing your result.
- **Reports**: send abuse reports to the address on our contact page. We review them promptly.
- **Enforcement**: for a violation we may suspend or close the wallet, key or account, refuse further requests, and where required by law, report to the relevant authorities and preserve evidence. Credits on a closed account for a serious violation may be forfeited to the extent the law allows.

## Operators

Running an image node means you agree to the rules above. You can stop serving images at any time by restarting your node without the image options. If you receive a request you believe is illegal, stop and report it; do not store it.

## Changes

We may update these rules as the law and the models change. The date of the last change is shown on this page once it is public.
