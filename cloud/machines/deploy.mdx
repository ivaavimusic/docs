---
title: Deploy a Machine
description: Launch a cloud instance in minutes — pick a plan, add an SSH key, and pay per hour.
---

Deploying a machine takes a minute. Do it from the dashboard under **Machines → Deploy**.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan + OS">Choose a server plan and OS image from the [catalog](/cloud/machines/catalog).</Step>
  <Step title="Add an SSH key">Bring your own public key (BYOK) or generate a new keypair in the browser. You'll use this to SSH in.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how long">Machines from 1 hour, GPUs from 2 hours. You pay for that time up front.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to pay">Credits or x402 USDC (pick the chain).</Step>
  <Step title="Launch">Confirm and deploy. The instance provisions and becomes reachable shortly after.</Step>
</Steps>

## How long, and what it costs

You prepay for the time you pick, at the plan's hourly price.

| Rental length | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 1 day or more | The hourly price times the hours |
| Under 1 day (machines from 1 hour, GPUs and AI machines from 2 hours) | The hourly price **+15%** for machines, **+20%** for GPUs and AI machines |

The minimum is 1 hour for machines and 2 hours for GPUs and AI machines. Extending works the same way, with the same minimums: adding less than a day costs the hourly price +15% (machines) or +20% (GPUs and AI machines, even on a CPU plan), a day or more is the normal price. When the time runs out the machine is deleted, so extend before then to keep it. Agent Pods and Grid node machines are rented by the day.

Over the API, `GET /compute/plans` shows the offer (`hourly`, with `min_hours_by_kind`) and whether each plan is a GPU (`is_gpu`). A GPU for 1 hour is refused with a 400 before any payment. Send `max_total_usd` with a deploy to refuse any higher price (nothing is charged).

## SSH keys

- **BYOK** — paste your existing public key; you keep the private key.
- **Generate** — create an Ed25519 keypair in the browser; download the private key immediately (it isn't stored for you).

Your public key is installed on the instance at launch so you can connect:

```bash
ssh root@<instance-ip>
```

## After launch

- The instance IP appears in **My Instances**.
- Need a root password instead of a key? Fetch a **one-time password** (see [Manage](/cloud/machines/manage)).
- Don't need it anymore? **Destroy** it to stop billing.

<Note>Time is prepaid. Destroying a machine early does not refund the unused time, so pick a length that fits the job.</Note>
