Singularity Node is the desktop app for running AI on your own computer and, optionally, serving the
Singularity Layer Grid. It bundles local chat, voice, and image generation with the node service
described in [Node setup](/cloud/provide/node-setup). This page covers version 1.11.

## Image & Video

The Image & Video tab makes images on your own Mac (Apple silicon). Everything is generated and stored
locally.

- **Create.** Pick a model, type a prompt, and the image appears in a chat-style thread.
- **Edit with words.** Edit any picture by describing the change. Drag a picture in from Finder or paste one to start from it.
- **Upscale.** 2x, 4x, 2K, 4K, or 8K.
- **Remove background.** Cut-out and upscale can run while another image is being made.
- **Save as.** PNG, JPG, HEIC, or transparent PNG.
- **Model pills.** Choose the model and aspect ratio. Under advanced: images at once (1 to 4), steps, and seed.
- **Stop.** Stopping a generation keeps your prompt and marks it "Stopped".

The first use asks you to accept responsibility for local generation.

### Library

Every image is kept in a Library on your Mac. It has search, filters, and favourites (star). You can
select and delete several images at once. Hover actions on each image: edit, upscale, remove
background, and save.

### Models

Models are listed by what your Mac can run, and new models can arrive without an app update. Choose
**Keep the model ready** (also in Settings) to keep it loaded so the next image starts right away.

Windows and Linux with NVIDIA GPUs are coming soon. Video is next.

## Serve images on the Grid

Any Mac that can run the image model can serve pictures on the Grid. The model's own memory
requirement is the only limit. Providers keep 80% of what a job earns. The default price is
0.0015 USD per megapixel-step, so a 1024x1024 image is about 0.0063 USD.

Buyers use the [Grid image API](/cloud/grid/image), and all generation is subject to the
[content rules](/cloud/grid/image-policy). Provider setup and earnings work as described in
[Earnings](/cloud/provide/earnings).

## Recents

Recents holds your text and image chats. Image chats show an image icon and reopen where you left off.

- **Pin** chats to keep them at the top.
- **Groups.** Use **Move to group** or **New group** to organise chats into your own groups, and collapse groups you are not using.
- **Reorder.** Drag chats to reorder them or move them between groups.

## Pictures in chat

Vision models (Qwen2.5-VL 3B) accept pictures. Attach one with the picture button, paste it, or drop
it into the chat. The picture is sent downscaled, and saved chats keep only a thumbnail.

## Models and voice

Switching models runs in the background. Progress shows in the model pill, and a status indicator
appears on every tab and on the Overview.

Voice models added in 1.11: MOSS-TTS Nano, LongCat-AudioDiT 1B, VoxCPM2 2B, and Rumik 3B. Rumik 3B
covers Hindi and 21 more Indian languages. It is for non-commercial personal use only and is never
served to the Grid. Soprano 80M now appears in the model list as intended. For dictation, see
[Speech-to-text transcription](/cloud/grid/transcription).

## Window behaviour

Closing the window hides it. Click the Dock icon to bring it back.
