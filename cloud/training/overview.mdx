Training Lab fine-tunes a small open model on your own examples and gives it
back to you: a LoRA adapter, a score report that shows how much it learned, and
a downloadable GGUF when the conversion succeeds (`gguf_available`). You choose the data, the base model and a GPU block. We
rent the GPU, run the job, and shut it down when it finishes.

Singularity xMESH 0.8B was trained and evaluated in Training Lab.

## How a run works

1. **Data.** Upload a JSONL file of chat examples, or describe the behaviour you
   want and have Training Lab generate a dataset. A dataset must be approved
   (schema-checked) before it can be trained on.
2. **Model.** Choose a base model from the catalog (today Qwen 2.5 0.5B Instruct
   and Qwen 2.5 1.5B Instruct).
3. **GPU block.** Choose a GPU and a number of hours. The price is fixed before
   you pay: hours x the GPU's hourly training price.
4. **Training.** The run reports progress while it trains. Every run keeps an
   evaluation split so the score report is measured, not guessed.
5. **Results.** Download the adapter (and the GGUF when available), or deploy the model as a private
   AI Machine.

## Pricing and refunds

You pay for a **prepaid block**: the GPU hours you chose, at the hourly price in
the catalog. Finishing early or cancelling does not refund the rest of the
block.

If something on our side stops a run before it trains (the GPU cannot start,
the provider fails, the run never reports in), the charge is refunded to your
credits automatically.

Payments made over x402 are refunded the same way: as credits on the paying
wallet's account, never on-chain.
